AXIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who this applies to

AXIS is a personal application built for, and used only by, its developer. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the developer signs in to it.

What data it accesses

With the developer's explicit consent, AXIS accesses the developer's own Google account data through Google's OAuth permissions: Gmail (reading, and sending where the developer approves) and Google Calendar.

How the data is used

This data is used only to help the developer manage their own email and schedule. It is not used for advertising, sold, shared with third parties, or used to train machine learning models by the developer.

Where the data is stored

Data is processed and stored on the developer's own privately hosted server and is not published or made publicly accessible. Parts of a message may be processed by third-party AI service providers (such as Anthropic and Groq) when the developer asks AXIS to act on it; sensitive details are filtered out first where possible.

Google API Services

AXIS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

The developer can revoke AXIS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete stored data from their own server.
